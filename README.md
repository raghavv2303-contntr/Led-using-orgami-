                                                   ANTENNA CREW 

**1 .DESCRIPTION**   
             

*  **Designed a simple greeting card for women’s day using led and battery  .**

**2\.REQUIRED MATERIALS** 

*  **Colour paper**  
*  **Foil paper**  
* **Origami paper**  
* **Glue**  
* **Sketch**  
* **Scissor**  
* **Tape**  
* **Led**   
* **Battery**  
* **Pencil**  
* **Stick pen** 


 **3 .PROCEDURE**

*    **Take a colour sheet and fold  it as half .**  
*    **Place it over one by one and glue it .**  
*    **Draw a girl with a bouquet and cut it and glue it in the colour sheet .**  
*    **Decorate the colour paper with heart and flowers.**  
*    **Decorate a front page with different shades of colour paper .**  
*   **In the bouquet attach the led and connect it with battery .**   


**4 .NOTES**

*   **Avoid long distance with the battery and the led .**  
* **Avoid cross connection with the connection .**  
* **Avoid connecting two or more leds in battery.**  
* **Carefully connect the led and the battery using foil paper .**
